**Decision: ACCEPT with the amendments below.**

**Why:** The core design is sound. The server freezes the plan, theme counts stay balanced, guests get no theme question between captures, and prompts put likeness first. What's missing: the Chef rule is unclear, a theme used twice has no rule to make its two photos differ, and two of the new themes overlap or carry a risk of offending guests.

**Allocation**
1. **Give Chef its own rule.** It sits under one-pose but describes four-photo behaviour. Make it work in both modes. Tapping Chef clears any chosen themes, and tapping a theme clears Chef. The server picks 1 theme, or 4 different active themes. If fewer than 4 are active, use rule 1's balancing. The pick is frozen in the plan.
2. **Shuffle the four directions across the four photos.** Then a theme used twice in a 2- or 3-theme session also gets two different directions.
3. **Define "adjacent" on the printed sheet.** On a 2×2 grid, ABAB puts repeats edge to edge. Place repeats on the diagonals instead.
4. **Selection checks.** In one-pose mode, tapping a theme replaces the current one. The server rejects unknown, inactive or duplicate IDs, wrong counts, and Chef mixed with themes. It never swaps in a theme silently. After a refresh, the client drops picks that are no longer available. "Mixed" means 2 or more different themes in the plan.

**Identity (all themes)**
5. Also preserve the number of people, apparent age, skin tone, glasses, hearing aids, prostheses and head coverings. No masks, face paint, fangs, altered ears, skin recolouring, slimming or beautifying. Added hats or hoods must leave faces fully visible and hair recognisable.

**New themes**
6. **Sous cloche:** make it a non-food curio or collector display, so it can't blur into À croquer. Glass only around or behind the group, never between camera and faces. People look relaxed, never trapped.
7. **À croquer:** no domes or cake stands. People stand among giant treats. They are never on plates, in bowls, near cutlery or mouths, and never dressed as food.
8. **Drôles de bêtes:** fabric costumes (hoods, ears, plush tails) from an approved list (e.g., fox, cat, owl, bunny, tiger). Leave out animals used as insults (apes/monkeys, pigs, cows, donkeys). Pick animals regardless of how anyone looks.

**Prompt wording**
- "Keep every person exactly as photographed: same number, faces, expressions, age, skin tone, body shape, hair, glasses and mobility aids. Change only clothing, accessories and setting."
- "Joyful, family-friendly portrait: playful, never gory, armed or revealing."
- Describe placement limits by what should happen, not by what to avoid. For example, Sous cloche: "A giant, airy glass bell arches high behind the group; every face is clear and reflection-free."
